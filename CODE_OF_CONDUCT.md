# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone who participates in our community will be treated with respect and dignity, regardless of their background, identity, experience level, or any other characteristic.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping others learn and grow

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Offensive or derogatory comments related to personal characteristics
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Discussions and forums
- Chat channels
- In-person events and meetings
- Project documentation and comments

This Code of Conduct also applies to official project communications on behalf of the community.

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers who do not follow the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by project leadership.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it by:

1. Opening a private issue or discussion if the platform allows
2. Contacting project maintainers directly via email
3. Providing as much detail as you're comfortable sharing

We take all reports seriously and will respond promptly. We are committed to protecting the privacy of reporters.

## Consequences

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in:

- A private warning from community leaders
- Temporary suspension from community spaces
- Permanent removal from the project or community

## Questions?

If you have any questions about this Code of Conduct, please reach out to the project maintainers. We're here to help create a welcoming and inclusive community for everyone.
